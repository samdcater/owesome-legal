Owesome Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Owesome is an app where friends set tasks for themselves, submit photo proof, and have friends approve or deny it. It is operated by Samuel Cater, California, USA ("we", "us"). Questions: samcater.owesome@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show ads, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We do not use AI services on your content.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, or microphone audio.
  • You can delete your account and your data inside the app.
  • Notifications are optional, never used for marketing, and you choose which ones you get.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your precise or approximate location, your contacts or address book, microphone recordings, advertising identifiers, or analytics about how you use the app. The app asks for camera or photo access only when you choose to take or pick a picture.

Notifications

Owesome can send push notifications, for example when a friend sends you a friend request or assigns you a task, needs you to review proof, or posts in one of your group chats. We ask for permission only after you sign in. You stay in control: choose exactly which types you receive under Profile → Settings → Notifications, mute individual groups from the group's chat settings, or turn notifications off completely in iPhone Settings. Reminders shortly before one of your own tasks is due are scheduled on your phone itself.

To deliver a notification, its text (for example a friend's username, a task title, or the start of a chat message) and your device token pass through Apple's push notification service and our push provider, Expo. We never use notifications for marketing or advertising. Your device token is removed when you log out or delete your account.

How we use your data

We use your data only to operate Owesome: to provide the features above, send the notifications you choose to receive, keep accounts secure, enforce our Community Rules and Terms of Use, respond to reports, and meet legal obligations.

Who can see your data

  • Other users. Your tasks, proof photos, comments and chat are visible to the friends or group you choose when you post. Your username, profile photo and theme color are visible to your friends.
  • Our service providers. Your data is stored and processed by Supabase (database, file storage, authentication), hosted in the United States. Sign-in and reset emails are currently sent through Gmail (Google). Push notifications are delivered through Expo and Apple's push notification service. These providers act on our behalf and do not use your data for their own purposes beyond running the service.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect people from serious harm.
  • Us. We can access reported content and related account information when we investigate a report.

Reporting, blocking and moderation

You can report users, tasks, photos, comments and chat messages, and you can block users, from inside the app. Reports include a snapshot of the reported content so we can review it even if it is later deleted. We review reports and may remove content, suspend, or ban accounts. We keep moderation records (reports and actions taken) for safety, abuse prevention and legal reasons.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Settings → Blocked users, support, and delete account → Delete my account. Deletion is permanent. It removes your profile, tasks, proof photos, comments, chat messages and chat photos, friendships, notification settings and device token, and your sign-in account. Uploaded files are removed from our storage as part of deletion.

When you delete an individual photo or message, it is removed from the app immediately, and the stored file is removed from our storage within a few days. Groups with no remaining members are deleted automatically, together with their messages and photos, unless they still hold tasks posted by former members.

Some things may remain after you delete your account:

  • Group background and banner photos that were shared with a group may remain for that group.
  • Reports about you or made by you, and records of moderation actions, are kept for safety and legal reasons.
  • If your account was banned, a record of the banned email address is kept so the ban can be enforced.
  • Encrypted backups kept by our provider may contain your data for a limited time before they are overwritten.

Accounts that are never confirmed by email are deleted automatically after about 3 days. When an account is deleted, its username becomes available again.

Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your profile in the app, delete content you posted, block other users, and delete your account. You can turn notifications on or off by type, or mute a single group, under Profile → Settings → Notifications or in iPhone Settings. You may also contact us at the email above to ask about, correct, or delete your data. If you live in California, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising.

Security

We use industry-standard protections, including encrypted connections, hashed passwords, and access rules that limit who can read each record. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

Owesome is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Where data is processed

Our service providers process data in the United States. If you use the app from another country, your data will be transferred there.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
