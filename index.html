Owesome

Set it. Prove it. Or owe it.

Owesome is an iPhone app where friends set tasks for themselves, post photo proof, and let their friends approve or deny it. Friends can also give each other tasks. Miss a task, and your friends pick the punishment. Owesome is for ages 18 and up.
