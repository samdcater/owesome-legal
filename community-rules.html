Owesome Community Rules

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Owesome is for friends keeping each other honest and having fun. These rules keep it that way. They apply to everything you post: tasks, proof photos, comments, punishment suggestions, chat messages, profile photos, usernames, and group names and photos.

The golden rule: if a post could genuinely hurt, scare, embarrass, or endanger someone, don't post it.

Not allowed

  • Harassment and bullying. No insults, targeting, pile-ons, or repeated unwanted contact.
  • Hate. No content that attacks people for race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or similar traits.
  • Threats and violence. No threats, graphic violence, or encouraging anyone to hurt themselves or others.
  • Sexual content. No nudity, sexually explicit photos or messages, or sexual harassment.
  • Dangerous or illegal punishments and tasks. No punishments that involve drinking alcohol, drugs, risky stunts, dangerous dares, anything illegal, or anything meant to humiliate or injure. Never pressure anyone to do something that makes them unsafe or uncomfortable.
  • Using assigned tasks to bully. Don't assign tasks or choose punishments to harass, embarrass, or pressure someone. Anyone can decline a task from a friend, and you can block or report anyone who keeps sending them.
  • Other people's privacy. Don't post photos of people who haven't agreed, and don't share anyone's private information (address, phone, school, workplace).
  • Impersonation. Don't pretend to be someone else, and don't use a username that copies or mocks a real person.
  • Spam and scams. No advertising, promotions, links meant to trick people, or fake accounts.
  • Fake proof used to deceive. Playful cheating between friends is your business, but don't submit photos that are stolen, graphic, or someone else's.
  • Breaking the system. Don't try to get around filters, blocks, suspensions or bans, or create new accounts to dodge them.
  • Anything illegal, or content that infringes someone else's copyright.

Be kind when you review

Approving or denying proof is part of the game. Deny honestly, and don't use reviews, comments or punishment suggestions to attack someone.

If something is wrong

  • Report it. Use the report option on a user, task, photo, comment, or chat message. Pick a reason and submit.
  • Block anyone. Blocking stops them from interacting with you. Manage blocks in Profile → Settings → Blocked users, support, and delete account.
  • Contact us. samcater.owesome@gmail.com

What happens when rules are broken

We aim to review reports within 24 hours. Depending on how serious it is, we may remove the content, warn you, suspend your account for 1, 3, 7, or 30 days, or permanently ban it. Serious cases, such as threats, sexual content involving minors, or content that puts someone at risk, can lead to an immediate ban and, where required, a report to the authorities. Some words are blocked automatically in tasks, comments and punishment suggestions.

If you think we got it wrong, email us and we'll take another look.
